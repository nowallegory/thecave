Claim 9

Child attorneys are not free to properly represent their client.

This page summarizes a single claim in the Tree of Inquiry. Eventually every claim will have its own page generated automatically from the project's data.

Supports

  • Court-appointed attorneys depend on continued appointments.
  • Attorneys must maintain working relationships with judges.
  • Organizational incentives may conflict with client interests.

Evidence

  • Kings County Family Court Transcript
  • Children's Law Center Handbook
  • Interview with Parent A

Counterarguments

  • Many child attorneys act in good faith.
  • Some jurisdictions have stronger oversight.
  • Professional ethics rules are intended to prevent conflicts.
